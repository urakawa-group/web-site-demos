Demos · Spectroscopy

Modulation Excitation and Phase-Sensitive Detection

Switch a stimulus on and off, fold the cycles, demodulate. First how the transformation works on one signal, then the cases: from one band without delay to a sequence of species with a spectator.

1 · The Transformation One averaged period turned into the phase domain, five ways: multiply and integrate, the phasor, harmonic content, what an offset, drift or noise does, and the whole spectrum as a map. Six inputs, from the square wave itself to a response that rises fast and falls slowly. 2 · Cases Every setting on a control, down to each band and time constant. Nine stored cases, from one band that follows the stimulus at once to a sequence of species with a spectator: folding, the phase domain, time values, square-wave and even harmonics.
