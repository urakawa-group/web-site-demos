Rank: How Many Components

A series of spectra of a changing mixture is a matrix. Before its species can be resolved, someone has to decide how many there are. Three ways to ask, on the same data.

1 · SVD The data taken apart into components ordered by size: the scree plot with its noise level, loadings and scores, the matrix operation as blocks, and what is left. Twelve cases, from one species to rank deficiency (co-evolving or parallel products), a band that moves, and the rank counted by an F-test and by OPALS. 2 · PCA The same after the mean spectrum is taken off. Two points of the spectra as a cloud show what centring does to a direction; raw, centred and autoscaled side by side show what it does to the rank. 3 · EFA The rank in time: the SVD of growing parts of the data, forward and backward, the window of each species read off, and the profiles they give, against the true concentrations.
