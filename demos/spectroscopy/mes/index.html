Modulation excitation spectroscopy, in one minute

A catalyst bed is switched periodically between CO2 + H2 and H2. Three surface bands answer: a fast species, a slow species and a spectator that sits there and does nothing. Fold the cycles, multiply by a sine of phase φ and integrate: the spectator is gone, and the phase where each band is largest tells how late it is.
