About the Demos

Interactive demonstrations of methods used in the Urakawa group: spectroscopy, kinetics, reactors and instruments. They explain how a method works with simulated, illustrative numbers; they are not data from our experiments. Equations and statements carry citations to the literature, listed under each demo. Errors are possible, please check against the cited sources before relying on a number.

Privacy

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from another server: fonts and scripts come from this repository. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub, as the host, processes technical data of each visit such as the IP address. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
